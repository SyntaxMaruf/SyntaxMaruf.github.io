V4.6.2Latest27 November 2026

Reliable chart movement and Saturday weeks

  • Charts pause while hovered or touched and resume as soon as the pointer leaves or touch ends.
  • Removed the focus lock and increased the gentle scrolling speed slightly.
  • Month charts move on desktop and phone. Year charts move on phone only.
  • Weeks now run from Saturday to Friday across the diary and Reports. The first week includes the diary start date.
  • Previous and Next now browse earlier and later months and years, including periods without records.
  • Saved daily records, targets and calculations stay unchanged.
V4.6.127 November 2026

Compact charts with gentle movement

  • Study chart bars are slimmer and shorter. Font sizes stay the same.
  • Month charts slowly move forward and back on phone and desktop, with a one-second pause at each end.
  • Year charts move automatically on phones only. Manual scrolling remains available.
  • Movement pauses during interaction and follows Pause animation and reduced-motion preferences.
  • Saved records and report calculations stay unchanged.
V4.627 November 2026

Weekly, monthly and yearly reports

  • Choose Week, Month or Year in Reports. Previous and Next use soft blue buttons.
  • Period totals and averages include only finalized, non-archived days. Empty days do not lower the averages.
  • Year view shows average study achievement for each calendar month.
  • Daily archive shows the English weekday below each record date.
  • Friday uses Jumu’ah in Daily log, daily reports and JPEG downloads. Other days keep Dhuhr.
  • Updated phone and desktop layouts and the About guide. Saved diary records stay unchanged.
V4.5.1.126 November 2026

A clearer reading history button

  • View reading history now has a teal button style, a history icon and an opening indicator.
  • Added a full-width button on phones and a clear keyboard focus style.
  • Saved reading history and report calculations stay unchanged.
V4.5.125 November 2026

Remove an accidental empty revision

  • New books start with Reading only. Add Revision is a manual action.
  • Select an empty revision and use Remove Revision to return to Reading.
  • A removed empty revision stays hidden when the book is opened again. Its number can be used again.
  • Revisions with reading details cannot be removed. Use Close book to pause them and keep saved reports.
  • Added clear instructions for first-time users on phone and desktop.
V4.524 November 2026

Book reading and revision cycles

  • Add book and Close book revision now have distinct button colors.
  • Complete, pause and reopen reading stages without deleting saved sessions or earlier reports.
  • 25 November: The first pass is Reading, followed by Revision 1, Revision 2 and more. Reading opens first and stages remain in ascending order.
  • First reading completion is counted separately from completed revisions in reports and JPEG downloads.
  • Start new revisions of the same book. Choose a revision for each new session, even while another revision is unfinished.
  • Revision overview shows sessions, revisions started, revisions completed and completions on the report date.
  • Daily reports and JPEG downloads include revision numbers. Repeated pages count once in each revision progress.
  • Book page ranges and daily reading minutes are checked before saving.
  • Book cards use soft color themes and a clear open and edit indicator.
  • Remove book is at the top right beside the book title. The reading date stays on its own line.
  • Edit book details now has a pencil icon and a button style.
  • Updated the layout for phone and desktop. Existing reading records stay unchanged.
V4.424 November 2026

Multiple reading sessions, one book

  • Added Add reading session for separate page ranges, minutes and topics in the same book and day.
  • Saved sessions remain editable. Adding a new session keeps earlier readings unchanged.
  • Daily reports and JPEG downloads list every session and sum pages and minutes.
  • Total book completion still counts different pages once, even across overlapping sessions.
  • Book revision starts collapsed when returning to Daily log and after saving. Closing the panel keeps saved readings.
  • Older single-session records remain supported without automatic rewriting.
V4.324 November 2026

A clearer daily values panel

  • Turned View exact daily values into a blue button with an icon and an opening indicator.
  • Added a soft blue panel background, a clear table header and alternating row colors.
  • Kept the newest dates first, full history scrolling and keyboard access.
  • Adapted the button and table for phone and desktop. Saved data and calculations stay unchanged.
  • Added optional Book revision in Daily log. Register a book once, then record inclusive page ranges, reading minutes and an optional topic each day.
  • Saved book names carry to new days. Reading fields start fresh; books can be edited or removed without changing earlier saved days.
  • Added per-book daily goals, total pages, unique-page completion and reading history in Reports and JPEG downloads. Reading stays separate from self-study percentages.
V4.224 November 2026

A richer visual Overview

  • Added four distinct illustrations for study, routine, spending and saved days.
  • Added a workspace banner with a desk scene and subtle moving light.
  • Added a soft blurred color backdrop behind the chart while keeping its lines clear.
  • Kept readable card values and adapted the design for phone and desktop.
  • Saved data, finalized records and calculations are unchanged. Other diary pages keep their current layout.
  • Fixed Reports to follow the selected date’s week. Added clear empty-week guidance, date ranges and labeled Previous and Next controls.
  • Placed Development time first in About and clarified Stage at creation with distinct badge colors.
  • Daily logs from 24 November 2026 ask for a daily summary. Earlier dates keep the original reflection prompt and saved notes.
V4.124 November 2026

A focused Overview dashboard

  • Put daily metrics first and removed the large decorative welcome banner.
  • Added consistent typography, neutral surfaces, clear spacing and a restrained blue accent.
  • Refined the chart, hourly plan and recent records for phone and desktop.
  • Kept the daily motivation card in a smaller layout below the useful information.
  • Changed presentation only. Saved records, finalized days, targets and calculations are unchanged.
  • Other diary pages keep their existing design.
V4.024 November 2026

One clear plan for today

  • Merged Today’s plan and the hourly picture timeline into one panel.
  • Placed Today’s plan above Every hour has its own rhythm.
  • Made Today’s plan a larger heading and pinned View routine to the top-right corner on phone and desktop.
  • View routine opens the weekly planner at the top instead of keeping the Overview scroll position.
  • Replaced Your planned day with View routine. It opens today’s weekday and resets the routine effective date to today.
  • Removed the duplicate plan list and gave Recent days its own full-width panel.
  • Kept the live highlight, manual scrolling and phone and desktop positioning.
V3.924 November 2026

Your current hour, in focus

  • Added a current-hour highlight that moves when the hour changes.
  • The highlight follows the diary time zone.
  • The timeline follows the day while keeping all 24 hours in their original order. On phone, middle hours stay centered while the first and last three hours stay near their ends. On desktop, the first nine hours stay at the start, the last nine stay at the end, and middle hours stay centered. Manual scrolling stays available on phone and desktop.
  • Refined the highlight from teal to gold, then to a clear deep-blue outer frame.
  • Kept the original card size and content area unchanged.
  • Made the desktop frame thicker and matched its rotating blue glow to the phone animation.
V3.824 November 2026

A fresh graph day at midnight

  • The graph adds a new date at midnight in the diary time zone.
  • New days start with three 0% points. Saved values replace them.
  • The date refreshes while the diary is open and when you return to the tab.
  • Starting points do not create or finalize diary records. Earlier saved data stays unchanged.

Every update, in one place

  • Back to diary returns to the page where the history was opened.
  • Marked early versions and dates as reconstructed estimates.

  • Added soft sky-blue gradients. Navigation stops at the diary start and today.
