V3.9Latest24 November 2026

Your current hour, in focus

  • A clear teal border and soft glow mark the current activity card.
  • The highlight moves automatically when the hour changes in your diary time zone.
  • The timeline follows the day while keeping all 24 hours in their original order. Manual scrolling stays available on phone and desktop.
V3.824 November 2026

A fresh graph day at midnight

  • The graph adds a new date at midnight in the diary time zone.
  • New days start with three 0% points. Saved values replace them.
  • The date refreshes while the diary is open and when you return to the tab.
  • Starting points do not create or finalize diary records. Earlier saved data stays unchanged.
V3.723 November 2026

Every update, in one place

  • Added a clickable version badge to every diary page.
  • Added this version history with short notes and dates.
  • Back to diary returns to the page where the history was opened.
  • Marked early versions and dates as reconstructed estimates.
V3.623 November 2026

Browse reports one day at a time

  • Added Previous and Next below the report date.
  • Matched both buttons to the date selector width.
  • Added soft sky-blue gradients. Navigation stops at the diary start and today.
V3.522 November 2026

A cleaner routine toolbar

  • Aligned weekday buttons and Add / edit time with the date inputs.
  • Removed extra space after Saturday.
  • Later fixes kept this version: corrected Bengali Hijri month names across browsers.

Daily targets that keep their history
